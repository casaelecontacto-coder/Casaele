07 — Our story

Why we built Casa de ELE.

Most Spanish courses are built for someone in Madrid or Miami — dubbed textbook dialogues, grammar drills, teachers who've never had to explain the subjunctive to someone thinking in Hindi or Tamil first.

We built something else: small live classes, taught by teachers who show up every week, with material that treats Indian learners as the point, not an afterthought. Every regional accent counts, and questions get answered twice if they need to be.

We called it Casa de ELE — the house of ELE. Ele is our mascot, and he leads you through it: a school for lessons, a cinema couch for shows, a grammar grill for the technical stuff, a reading balcony, a recording room. Pick whichever door fits your Tuesday.

Same teaching, ten different doors in.
